Cheat Vault

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Cheat Vault is an unofficial cheat-code guide. We don't run accounts, analytics or servers of our own. This page explains which third parties receive data and why.

What we collect

We (the developer) collect nothing. There are no accounts or sign-up, and we never receive your name or email.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads and an occasional full-screen ad, served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (including Apple's IDFA, only if you allow tracking)
  • Advertising data, such as which ads you saw or tapped
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Diagnostic and performance data about the ad SDK

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) you are asked for consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice any time in Cheat Vault under Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS, the app asks permission before using your device's advertising identifier; you can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised.

Premium users see no ads, and the ad SDK is not started for them.

Purchases

Premium is sold through the App Store or Google Play, which process your payment. We never see your payment details. To check whether Premium is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random app user ID and your purchase receipts, under RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Cheat code updates

On launch the app downloads the latest list of cheat codes from GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log your IP address for this request under GitHub's Privacy Statement. No other data is sent.

What stays on your device

Your favorites and selected platform are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

Cheat Vault is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
